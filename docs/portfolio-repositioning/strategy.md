# Positioning Strategy

## Primary identity

**AI Product & Platform Engineering Leader**

Supporting value proposition:

> I build customer-facing AI products, agentic platforms, and AI-native delivery systems—from product discovery and architecture through evaluation, production, and adoption.

This identity is singular. Adjacent role credibility should emerge from evidence rather than a list of titles.

## Narrative pillars

### 1. Build AI Products

Show user problems, product surfaces, ownership, human control, maturity, and public proof before technical implementation detail.

### 2. Engineer AI Platforms

Show RAG, agent orchestration, authorization, evaluation, observability, fail-soft behavior, security boundaries, and governed delivery systems.

### 3. Lead Transformation

Connect technical direction to teams, platform ownership, delivery systems, adoption, governance, and historical enterprise automation impact.

## Audience decision

The first 20 seconds should establish credibility for Principal/Lead AI Engineering, AI Product Engineering leadership, Head of AI, Platform Architecture leadership, and AI-centered CTO paths. The site will not present these as competing headlines.

## Evidence hierarchy

1. Verified product and role evidence with explicit maturity.
2. Public repository or live demo evidence.
3. Local portfolio/resume records with clear context.
4. Qualitative product decisions when metrics are unavailable.
5. Honest limitations and next stage.

Unsupported or conflicting claims are removed, downgraded, or labeled as concept/developer preview/in development.

## Homepage product priority

Provisional order pending claim normalization:

1. Rice Executive AI Assistant — strongest local 0→1, multi-surface, approval-gated product evidence; pilot maturity.
2. LA Muni RAG / procedure workflow advisor — public repository evidence to be inspected and normalized.
3. AI Recruiting Copilot — a clearly labeled mock-data demo with recruiter-controlled review.
4. Harness SDLC — differentiated platform methodology, with public status aligned to `spec_ready` rather than “implemented”.

TimeEstimator moves to the AI Platforms & Developer Tooling / product lab layer as a live developer preview. ConstructHub remains visible as an infrastructure-validated concept/staging demo until the public repository contains credible product artifacts.

## Content principles

- Lead with user, problem, product, role, maturity, and proof.
- Move stack, commands, status matrices, and endpoint detail behind progressive disclosure.
- Separate AI product delivery evidence from historical automation/transformation metrics.
- Treat Business Psychology as Human Systems and adoption expertise, not clinical expertise.
- Preserve chemistry styling as a signature accent, never as a substitute for product clarity.
